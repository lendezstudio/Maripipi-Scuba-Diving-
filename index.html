The island

There’s more to Maripipi than the shoreline.

Maripipi is an island municipality in Biliran, Philippines. Its coastal setting invites you to slow down, look closer, and discover a different side of island life.

For Maripipi Scuba Diving, that sense of discovery comes with a purpose: helping visitors appreciate the islands and care about their future.

Explore with purpose

Discover the beauty. Care for what makes it special.

A little care goes a long way underwater.

Respect for the sea is part of a thoughtful diving experience.

  • i.

    Look without disturbing.

    Give marine life space. Avoid touching, chasing, or feeding animals.

  • ii.

    Leave the reef as you find it.

    Do not stand on coral or collect marine souvenirs.

  • iii.

    Be aware of your surroundings.

    Follow your dive professional’s guidance and keep yourself and your equipment clear of the reef.

  • iv.

    Keep the islands clean.

    Take your rubbish with you and reduce unnecessary single-use waste.

Before you take the next step.

Can I inquire if I have never tried scuba diving?

Yes. Let the team know you are new to scuba and ask which introductory options, if any, are available. Participation requirements and suitability need to be confirmed before booking.

What should certified divers include in an inquiry?

Share your certification level, approximate date of your last dive, preferred travel dates, and group size. This gives the team a useful starting point for discussing your plans.

Where will we dive?

Ask the team which locations may be available for your dates and experience level. Do not assume a specific site or route is included until it has been confirmed.

Are equipment and boat arrangements included?

Confirm this when you inquire. Ask for a clear breakdown of equipment, boat arrangements, fees, and any other inclusions before agreeing to a trip.

How do I get to Maripipi?

Maripipi is in Biliran, Philippines. Confirm your arrival route, current transport schedules, and the meeting point before you travel.

Can weather affect my plans?

Yes. Sea and weather conditions can affect diving and boat travel. Confirm the operator’s arrangements for schedule changes, cancellations, and payments before booking.

How much does diving cost?

Contact the team for current rates and confirmed inclusions based on the experience and arrangements you are asking about.
